TakeBack

Genre — 2D TopDown Simulation Role — Project Manager & Designer

TAKE BACK is a 2D top down game that tells the story of a boy named Risky, a high school student who has a careless nature. In this game, Risky's lunch box is left at school and he has to get it back before his mother finds out.
