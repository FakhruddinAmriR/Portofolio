Campus Chronicles

Genre — TBD Role — Game Designer & Game Programmer

CAMPUS CHRONICLES is a 2D platformer game that puts players in the shoes of a new student at the University of Jember. Players can explore the campus and view its buildings, accompanied by an AI named B.O.S.C.A.

My Role

As both the game designer and programmer for this game, I was responsible for the overall game design and the actual development process. I created and defined the game loop, and designed the gameplay flow as well as the minigames. I also implemented character movement, menu systems, dialogue, and more. Since I handled both design and implementation, I also conducted testing on the systems resulting from the ideas I had developed.
